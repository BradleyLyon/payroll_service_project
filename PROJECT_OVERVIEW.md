## What this project is
Lead generation + research service for Simon, an account overseer at Heartland (a Global Payments company). Simon retains existing clients and finds new ones. We find and qualify leads; his team handles all outreach and closing. We never close.

## The core constraint
Simon has a full team to work leads — **quantity is unlimited, quality is the product.** Leads must be enriched and qualified well enough that his team prefers our list over their own research or other sources. Past lead lists failed because they were too thin to act on.

## Targets
- Closure rate goal on our leads: **3–5%**
- Delivery goal: ranked, enriched batches (pilot A → pilot B → 5–20/week ongoing)

## Ideal customer profile
<!-- This is the ranking rubric's foundation. Refine after Simon's pilot feedback. -->
- **Sweet spot:** established restaurants with multiple locations, up to ~50 employees. Big enough to make money (payroll, volume), small enough to not be locked into enterprise contracts.
- **Also strong:** pre-opening spots (new liquor license, no website/DoorDash yet — nothing configured, Simon can bundle website + DoorDash as the hook).
- **Disqualify:** large corporate chains (McDonald's, Chipotle tier) — already locked in. Brand-controlled franchises: check which services are franchise-controlled before qualifying.

## Known close rates (from Simon)
- CPA/accountant referrals: ~95%
- Warm referrals: ~75%
- Cold qualified leads (new opening, leadership change): 15–20%

## Geography
NYC (we can visit in person — our edge). Simon can serve any state remotely.

## What we deliver per lead
<!-- Full spec lives in LEAD_SPEC.md -->
Business name, contact, size estimate, platform/hosting detected, website/DoorDash status, one compliance or pain-point flag, one-line pitch angle, rank.
